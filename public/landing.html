choice · score · noul — with calibrated confidence.

Three primitives. Most of the agent loop.

Route models, gate risky tools, triage tickets, score risk—without spending a frontier LLM call on every tiny judgment.

  • choice

    Pick one of up to 255 labelled options. Returns the winning key, per-option probabilities, and confidence.

  • score

    Place input on a 2–10 level spectrum. Get a fractional score plus the full distribution—ready to threshold.

  • noul

    A calibrated yes/no as a probability from 0 to 1. Ideal for guardrails, filters, and auto-approve / escalate gates.
